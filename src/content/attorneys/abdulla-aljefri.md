“40+ government and institutional entities”.

That number represents something deeper than a client list.

It represents trust, built over years of delivering counsel that is not just legally sound, but strategically meaningful.

Abdulla Aljefri, Co-Founder and Managing Partner of ALSA Law, has spent his career at the intersection of law, policy, and statecraft.

A graduate of the Harvard Kennedy School with a degree in Public Policy, Abdulla spent 17 years at the Abu Dhabi Judicial Department (ADJD), rising to Senior Government Attorney. It is a tenure that gave him something no private practice career can replicate: an authoritative understanding of the UAE's legal and judicial system, built from within its most important institution.

That foundation took him further still, representing the interests of the United Arab Emirates at the highest echelons of international advocacy, carrying the weight of national interest with the discipline and distinction that defines everything he does.

At ALSA Law, Abdulla brings all of that to every client he serves: personally, deliberately, and with the full weight of a career built at the highest levels of this nation's legal life.
